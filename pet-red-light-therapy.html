Before the First Session

Consultation required before treatment.

To protect the health and safety of your pet — and in accordance with Florida law — all dogs must first complete an initial consultation and evaluation with Dr. Aimee Burke of Fur Ever Friends Veterinary Clinic before beginning any therapy.

Dr. Burke will then work with our team to draft a customized treatment plan tailored to your dog’s needs.

Good to Know

Pet wellness questions.

Can I stay with my dog?

Yes. The experience is designed to feel calm and supportive, and the Heal Together package lets you recover side by side.

Does every dog need a consultation?

Yes. Every dog begins with a consultation and evaluation by Dr. Aimee Burke of Fur Ever Friends Veterinary Clinic, who works with our team on a personalized plan.

Does this replace regular veterinary care?

No. Our red light therapy supports your dog’s comfort and recovery and works alongside regular veterinary care.

Provided under the guidance of a licensed veterinarian. Red light therapy supports your dog’s comfort and recovery alongside — not in place of — regular veterinary care.
