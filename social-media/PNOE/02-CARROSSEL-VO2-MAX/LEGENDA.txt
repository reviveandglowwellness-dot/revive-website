How strong is your aerobic engine?

VO₂ Max shows how effectively your body takes in and uses oxygen during exercise. It is one of the clearest measures of aerobic fitness — and it is highly trainable.

Elite soccer players often measure between 55–65 mL/kg/min, while many recreational athletes fall in the mid-40s to low-50s. But the goal is not simply to compare yourself with an athlete. The real value is understanding your own starting point so you can train, recover, and progress with greater direction.
