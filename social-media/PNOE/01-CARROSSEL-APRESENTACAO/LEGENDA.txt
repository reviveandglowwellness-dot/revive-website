What if one breath test could help you understand how your body actually uses oxygen and produces energy?

PNOĒ Metabolic Testing goes beyond estimates. Through a guided breath analysis, it provides a detailed look at key markers such as your VO₂ Max, resting metabolic rate, fuel utilization, cardiovascular fitness, and personalized training zones.

This information can help bring greater direction to goals such as:

• improving energy and fitness
• supporting weight-management strategies
• training and recovering more intentionally
• understanding metabolic plateaus
• building a more personalized longevity plan

Your results become a starting point for a wellness plan built around your body, your data, and your goals.
