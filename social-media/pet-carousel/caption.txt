Wellness is for the whole family — including the four-legged members. 🐾

Revive & Glow will offer veterinarian-guided red light therapy designed to support your dog’s comfort, mobility, and recovery in a calm, welcoming environment.

Here’s how the process works:

1️⃣ Every dog begins with an initial consultation and evaluation with Dr. Aimee Burke of Fur Ever Friends Veterinary Clinic.

2️⃣ Dr. Burke works with our team to create a personalized treatment plan based on your dog’s individual needs.

3️⃣ Once the plan is established, your dog can begin gentle red light therapy sessions at Revive & Glow.

Red light therapy works alongside your dog’s regular veterinary care — not in place of it. Our goal is to provide thoughtful support so your best friend can feel their best and keep enjoying everything they love.
