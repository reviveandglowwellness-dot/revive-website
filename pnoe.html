One Test, A Full Picture

What PNŌĒ reveals about you

You breathe into a mask while we analyze the air you exhale. From that, PNŌĒ maps the metrics that drive energy, weight, performance, and healthy aging.

🫁

VO₂ Max

Your cardiorespiratory fitness — how well your body delivers and uses oxygen. One of the most studied markers of overall fitness and healthy aging.

🔥

Resting Metabolic Rate

Exactly how many calories your body burns at rest — the real number to build nutrition around, instead of a generic online estimate.

⚖️

Fat vs. Carb Burning

Whether you burn mostly fat or carbs at rest and in motion — the key to smarter fat-burning, energy, and endurance.

❤️

Heart & Training Zones

Your personalized heart-rate zones, so every workout — easy or hard — lands exactly where it should for the result you want.

🌬️

Breathing Efficiency

How efficiently you breathe under effort — an often-overlooked driver of energy, stamina, and recovery.

⏳

Metabolic Fitness Age

A single, motivating snapshot of how your metabolism and fitness compare to your calendar age — and a baseline to improve.

Why It Matters

The number longevity experts watch most.

Cardiorespiratory fitness — your VO₂ max — is one of the strongest, most-studied signals of long-term health and vitality. The encouraging part: it's trainable. You can't improve what you don't measure, and PNŌĒ gives you the real starting line.

Pair your results with our recovery therapies, coaching, and body-composition tracking, and you have a complete loop: measure, move, recover, and watch the numbers move with you.
