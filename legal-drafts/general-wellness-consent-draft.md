## 5. General Risks and Assumption of Inherent Risk

I understand that wellness and recovery services involve risks that cannot be eliminated completely, even when reasonable precautions are used. General risks may include:

- dizziness, lightheadedness, fainting, headache, fatigue, or nausea;
- temporary changes in heart rate, blood pressure, circulation, or body temperature;
- dehydration or overheating;
- cold stress, shivering, numbness, or discomfort;
- skin redness, irritation, sensitivity, or temporary changes in sensation;
- muscle soreness, cramping, bruising, or aggravation of an existing condition;
- slips, trips, falls, or loss of balance;
- equipment malfunction or unexpected individual response; and
- in rare circumstances, serious injury or a medical emergency.

The risks and contraindications unique to a selected service will be presented in a separate service-specific consent before that service.

I voluntarily choose to participate after having the opportunity to ask questions. I accept the inherent risks of the services I choose, subject to the limitations of applicable law.
