Multi-Wavelength Light Therapy

PlatinumLED BioMax

One advanced panel with blue, red, and near-infrared wavelengths — each reaching a different depth.

Blue Light · 480 nmSurface-level light that may support clearer-looking skin and overall skin wellness.
Red Light · 630 + 660 nmVisible light that may support cellular energy, healthy-looking skin, and natural repair processes.
Near-Infrared · 810 + 830 + 850 + 1060 nmInvisible, deeper-reaching light that may support muscle recovery, mobility, and performance routines.
Learn more

What it is. Photobiomodulation uses non-UV blue, red, and near-infrared LED light. Each wavelength reaches a different depth, allowing the experience to be selected around your goals and comfort.

How it works. Red and near-infrared light interact with light-sensitive structures in cells. Blue light acts closer to the skin's surface. The response depends on wavelength, dose, treatment area, and the person.

What research has found. Research on localized photobiomodulation reports potential benefits in specific settings, but results cannot automatically be applied to every device or full-body session.

Whole-body evidence. A 2025 review found only five small studies and no demonstrated improvement in exercise performance or recovery biomarkers, so we do not promise those outcomes.

Who may be interested. Adults seeking a brief, noninvasive addition to a skin-wellness, relaxation, or active-lifestyle routine.

What to expect. Relax in front of our multi-wavelength LED panels for a 20-minute session. Protective eyewear is provided when appropriate, and there is no downtime.

Important. This is a general wellness service, not medical treatment. Results vary. Tell our team about light sensitivity, eye conditions, pregnancy, or medicines and skincare products that increase sensitivity to light before your session.

Research sources:
Álvarez-Martínez & Borden, Lasers in Medical Science, 2025 · Li et al., Lasers in Medical Science, 2024

Infrared Sauna

Clearlight Sanctuary 2

Deep, gentle heat to relax muscles, unwind, and sweat it out.

Learn more

What it is. An infrared sauna uses radiant heat to warm the body in a private cabin, generally with a different heat experience from a traditional hot-air sauna.

How it works. Infrared energy is absorbed at the body surface and converted to heat. Your temperature and heart rate may rise as you warm and sweat.

What research has found. Reviews describe possible benefits from regular sauna bathing, but studies vary widely and many are small. Evidence specific to infrared saunas is more limited than the marketing claims often suggest.

Who may be interested. Adults looking for warmth, quiet relaxation, and a comfortable complement to an active recovery routine.

What to expect. Sit back in a private Clearlight sauna, hydrate, and leave whenever you need to. A typical session is about 20–40 minutes and can be shortened for first-time guests.

Good to know. Sweating helps the body regulate temperature; we do not describe sauna sessions as a detox or promise a specific calorie burn.

Important. Sauna is a general wellness experience. Ask your healthcare provider first if you are pregnant, have cardiovascular or blood-pressure concerns, take medicines that affect heat tolerance, or are unsure whether heat exposure is appropriate. Do not use it while ill, dehydrated, or impaired by alcohol or drugs.

Research source:
Hussain & Cohen, Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine, 2018

Cold Plunge

Nordic Wave

An invigorating cold reset for circulation, focus, and resilience.

Learn more

What it is. Cold-water immersion is a short, supervised exposure to cool water that creates an immediate cold sensation and a strong, temporary physiological response.

How it works. Cold causes blood vessels near the skin to narrow and can quickly change breathing, heart rate, and blood pressure. That is why a gradual entry and calm breathing matter.

What research has found. A 2023 review of 68 studies found that post-exercise effects varied by the type of exercise, timing, and outcome measured. Some recovery measures improved, while some short-term sprint and jump measures worsened.

Who may be interested. Healthy adults who enjoy an invigorating challenge or want to explore cold exposure as part of a broader recovery routine.

What to expect. Enter slowly, focus on a steady exhale, and leave whenever you want. First sessions are supervised. Typical exposure is about 2–5 minutes at approximately 50–59°F; shorter is always acceptable.

Contrast option. Cold plunge can follow the infrared sauna, but contrast is optional and is not appropriate for everyone.

Important. Cold immersion places acute stress on the cardiovascular system. Do not participate without medical clearance if you have heart or circulation concerns, uncontrolled high blood pressure, Raynaud's disease, are pregnant, or are unsure whether cold exposure is appropriate.

Research source:
Moore et al., Sports Medicine, 2023

Compression Therapy

Normatec

Dynamic compression boots for a comfortable, massage-like recovery ritual.

Learn more

What it is. Normatec boots use intermittent pneumatic compression: overlapping chambers fill and release in sequence along the legs.

How it works. The changing pressure creates a massage-like squeezing pattern. Pressure is adjustable and should feel snug, never painful or numb.

What research has found. Studies in endurance athletes suggest possible short-term relief in perceived soreness, but do not show consistent, lasting improvement in exercise-induced muscle damage.

Who may be interested. Runners, athletes, travelers, and anyone who wants a passive, comfortable recovery ritual after time on their feet.

What to expect. We fit the boots and choose a comfortable pressure. Then you can read, rest, or listen to music for about 20–30 minutes.

Good to know. Compression is not a treatment for varicose veins, lymphedema, blood clots, or an injury, and we do not claim that it removes lactic acid.

Important. Ask a healthcare provider before use if you have or may have a blood clot, circulation disorder, significant swelling, an open wound, fracture, infection, or recent surgery affecting the legs.

Research sources:
Stedge & Armstrong, Journal of Sport Rehabilitation, 2021 · Systematic review and meta-analysis, 2025

Whole Body Vibration

Movement & Active Aging

A brief, low-impact platform experience designed to complement movement, stability, and an active lifestyle.

Learn more

What it is. Whole-body vibration uses a platform that creates rapid, controlled movement beneath your feet.

How it works. As the platform moves, your body makes small, continuous muscular adjustments to help maintain posture and stability.

What research has found. A 2026 systematic review of 36 studies in adults age 60 and older found the most consistent improvements in lower-body strength. Results for balance and physical function were promising, but varied between studies.

Another 2026 analysis. Six randomized trials involving 202 older adults with sarcopenia reported moderate improvements in lower-body strength and physical performance, but no significant increase in muscle mass.

Who may be interested. Adults looking for a brief, low-impact complement to regular movement, strength, mobility, or active-aging routines. Research does not establish it as a replacement for conventional exercise.

What to expect. Our team helps you choose a comfortable stance or simple supported position on the platform. Sessions are brief and personalized to your comfort.

Important. Whole-body vibration is a general wellness experience, not medical treatment or physical therapy. Results vary. Ask your healthcare provider before beginning if you have a health condition, current injury, recent procedure, are pregnant, or are unsure whether vibration is appropriate for you.

Research sources:
Dong et al., JAMDA, 2026 · Beom et al., Scientific Reports, 2026 · Peng et al., PeerJ, 2025

Grounding

Restore & Reconnect

A calming practice to settle the nervous system and reconnect.

Learn more

What it is. A quiet, grounding practice designed to help you slow down and reconnect.

What we can honestly say. The value of this part of the visit is the pause itself: a comfortable setting, fewer distractions, and a few unhurried minutes after your session.

About the evidence. Grounding is often promoted with electrical or medical explanations, but current research is limited and does not justify presenting those claims as established fact.

Who may be interested. Anyone who wants a quiet transition before returning to the rest of the day.

What to expect. Sit or rest comfortably, breathe naturally, and take a few minutes without a timer or performance goal.

Pairs well with. Grounding can close any Revive Method™ visit, especially after vibration, sauna, or compression.

Important. We offer grounding as a relaxation ritual, not as a treatment and not as a way to “balance” electrical charge, hormones, inflammation, or the nervous system.

Hydrogen Water

Hydrate & Replenish

Fresh hydrogen-infused water to rehydrate and refresh after your session.

Learn more

What it is. Drinking water with dissolved molecular hydrogen, offered as a refreshing hydration option after your session.

How it is different. It is ordinary water infused with hydrogen gas. The dissolved hydrogen gradually escapes after preparation, so it is best enjoyed fresh.

What research has found. Human studies exist, but they are generally small and examine varied populations and outcomes. Recent reviews call for larger, better-controlled trials.

Who may be interested. Guests who want to rehydrate after sauna, cold exposure, exercise, or any Revive Method™ visit.

What to expect. Simply drink it as you would regular water. There is no special protocol and no promised sensation or result.

Good to know. Hydrogen water does not replace regular hydration, balanced nutrition, medical care, or prescribed treatment.

Important. We serve it as a hydration amenity. We do not claim that it detoxifies, alkalizes the body, slows aging, or treats a health condition.

Research source:
Ostojic et al., systematic review, 2023

Body Composition

Tanita RD-545

Establish a baseline and follow body-composition trends over time.

Learn more

What it is. A quick, noninvasive bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA) using our Tanita RD-545. It estimates body-composition measures such as body fat, muscle mass, visceral-fat rating, total body water, and metabolic age.

How it works. The analyzer sends a very small electrical current through the body and uses the measured resistance, together with profile information, to estimate body composition.

Why repeat measurements matter. A consistent series can be more useful than a single number. We use it to establish a baseline and observe trends alongside your goals.

What affects a reading. Hydration, food, recent exercise, time of day, skin temperature, and measurement conditions can change BIA estimates. Similar preparation each time makes comparisons more meaningful.

What to expect. You stand barefoot on the analyzer and hold its hand grips for a few minutes. Results are reviewed privately and never compared with another guest's.

Good to know. The assessment is complimentary on your first visit. Your results are estimates, not a grade or a guarantee of future change.

Important. BIA is not a diagnosis and does not replace laboratory testing, medical imaging, or advice from a licensed healthcare professional. Tell our team if you have an implanted electronic medical device or are pregnant before using the analyzer.

Research sources:
Marra et al., Critical Overview of BIA and DXA, 2019 · Prado et al., Methodological Standards, 2026

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Revive & Glow Wellness?

A boutique recovery and longevity studio in Pinellas Park, FL. We bring multi-wavelength light therapy, infrared sauna, cold plunge, compression, whole body vibration, and more under one roof — designed to help you recover, restore, and feel your best.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Sessions are by appointment so we can give you a calm, personalized experience. You can reserve your visit online through our secure Mindbody booking page.

Is this medical treatment?

No. Our services are for general wellness and relaxation. They are not medical care and are not intended to diagnose, treat, or cure any condition. Please consult your doctor before starting any new wellness routine.

What is the complimentary wellness assessment?

Every journey starts with a body composition scan on our Tanita analyzer, plus AI-guided recommendations — so your plan is built around your goals and you can track real progress over time.

What can I use a Revive Session for?

Each Revive Session may be used for one Red Light Therapy, Infrared Sauna, Cold Plunge, or Compression Therapy visit. Whole Body Vibration & Grounding is available separately for $29.

Do unused membership sessions roll over?

No. Membership sessions renew each billing month and must be used within that billing cycle. Unused sessions expire and do not carry into the next month.

How does the Revive Elite guest pass work?

Elite members receive one guest pass each billing month. It may be used for any therapy included as a Revive Session and expires at the end of that billing cycle.

What is the Revive Elite pet wellness perk?

Elite members receive one Pet Red Light Therapy Session each billing month. The session expires at the end of the billing cycle and does not roll over. Pet sessions support general wellness and should follow appropriate veterinary guidance.

Are you open?

Yes. Our Pinellas Park studio is open, and appointments can be reserved through our online booking page.
