Signature Therapies

One Revive Session. One therapy. Chosen around you.

Each Revive Session may be used for Multi-Wavelength Light Therapy, Infrared Sauna, Cold Plunge, or Compression Therapy.

20 minutesMulti-Wavelength Light TherapyBlue, red, and near-infrared light in one calm, noninvasive session.

What to expect: Relax in front of our PlatinumLED BioMax panels. We help select the wavelengths around your goals and comfort, and provide protective eyewear when appropriate.

This is a general wellness service, not medical treatment. Tell our team about light sensitivity, eye conditions, pregnancy, or products and medicines that increase light sensitivity.

20–40 minutesInfrared SaunaGentle, enveloping heat for quiet restoration and an unhurried reset.

What to expect: Your enclosed sauna and cold plunge room is reserved for you, or for someone you choose to bring. First visits can be shortened for comfort.

Ask your healthcare provider first if you are pregnant, have cardiovascular or blood-pressure concerns, take medicines that affect heat tolerance, or are unsure whether heat exposure is appropriate.

2–5 minutes exposureCold PlungeA brief, guided cold-water experience for an invigorating reset.

What to expect: Enter gradually, focus on a steady exhale, and leave whenever you want. First sessions are supervised and shorter is always acceptable.

Cold immersion places acute stress on the cardiovascular system. Medical clearance is important for heart or circulation concerns, uncontrolled high blood pressure, Raynaud's disease, pregnancy, or uncertainty about cold exposure.

15–30 minutesCompression TherapyComfortable, massage-like compression for passive recovery.

What to expect: We fit the Normatec boots and select a comfortable pressure. Compression is offered in an open area near reception.

Ask a healthcare provider before use if you have or may have a blood clot, circulation disorder, significant swelling, an open wound, fracture, infection, or recent surgery affecting the legs.

Good to Know

Before your first visit.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are available Monday–Saturday, 8 AM–8 PM, and Sunday, 10 AM–4 PM. Walk-ins are welcome daily, 10 AM–4 PM, for a $39 Single Session, subject to availability.

What can I use a Revive Session for?

One Revive Session may be used for Multi-Wavelength Light Therapy, Infrared Sauna, Cold Plunge, or Compression Therapy. Whole Body Vibration & Grounding is available separately for $29.

Are sessions private or shared?

Your therapy is never shared with strangers. Sauna, cold plunge, and light therapy take place in rooms reserved for you. Compression and vibration are offered in open areas, and other guests may be enjoying different therapies elsewhere in the studio.

Is this medical treatment?

No. Our services support general wellness and relaxation. They are not medical care and are not intended to diagnose, treat, or cure any condition.
